1. Introduction

Sri Sakthi Calendar ("we", "our", "us") is a Tamil calendar app developed by Netkathir Technology. It shows daily Panchangam details such as the Tamil date, Nal, Natchathiram, Thithi, Yogam, Karanam, Rahu Kalam, Emagandam, Vasthu days, festivals, auspicious timings and house-plot measurement matching. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or address. We do not run our own server, and the app does not send your data to us.

Any dates, times, plot sizes or city selections you enter are used only on your device to calculate results. They are not saved or transmitted by us.

3. Location

The app does not access your device location. Sunrise and sunset based timings are calculated using built-in coordinates of the city you choose from the list inside the app.

4. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Through the AdMob SDK, Google may automatically collect and use:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (such as model, OS version and language)
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data

This is used to show ads, including personalised ads, and to measure ad performance. To learn how Google uses data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services

5. Managing Ad Personalisation

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Sharing and Phone Features

  • If you tap the share button, the content you choose is passed to the app you select in your device's share menu. We do not receive it.
  • If you tap the business phone number, the app opens your phone's dialer. No call is made without your action.

7. How We Use Information

Information collected by Google AdMob is used to display advertisements and keep the app free for users. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Security

Because the app does not collect or store your personal data, there is no personal data held by us to protect or delete. Ad data handled by Google is transmitted over secure connections.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

10. Accuracy of Content

The Panchangam, Nal, Natchathiram, Vasthu and other details in this app are provided for general information and cultural reference only. Calculations follow the Vakkiya method and may differ by a few minutes from printed calendars. Please consult a qualified person for important religious or personal decisions.
